The problem

Your unhappiest customers already left a review. Your happiest ones haven't.

An unhappy customer is motivated to complain right away. A happy customer means to say something nice — then gets busy and forgets. That gap is where most of your best reviews are quietly lost.

"Please leave us a review" — said out loud, then forgotten by the time the customer gets home.

Leaving a review the normal way means opening a browser, searching your business name, finding the right listing, then scrolling to "Write a review" — real friction between meaning to and actually doing it.

A review link sent by WhatsApp, buried under the day's other messages within minutes.

Every extra step between "happy customer" and "posted review" is a chance for the review to never happen.

A review card at your counter turns that whole search into one tap — asked at the exact moment they're happiest, right after the haircut, the meal, or the service.
